Keyboard shortcuts

On this page

Find a unit
  1. Scan the label, or type the ID in Search or scan (Ctrl K).
  2. One match opens the unit. More than one shows a list.
  3. To narrow the list, use the filter chips: project, place or stage.
 Move units
  1. Choose Move storage units, or press Ctrl K and type Move.
  2. Scan the units and the shelf, in either order.
  3. Check the list, then choose Move. Undo stays in the message for 5 seconds.

A unit's ID stays the same wherever it goes.

 Print labels
  1. Select the units, then choose Print labels.
  2. Pick the printer if this computer has more than one.
  3. If a label didn't print, open the unit and choose Print again.

Getting around

How the sidebar works
  1. Today, Projects and Inbox are always at the top.
  2. Click a section's name to fold it. Recent starts folded.
  3. To reorder or hide sections, choose Customize sidebar at the bottom of the list, then Done.
  4. Press Ctrl B to collapse the sidebar to icons.

Your layout is saved for you, so it comes back the same on any office computer you sign in to.

 Search or scan anythingPress Ctrl K, then type an ID, a project number or a page, or scan a label. One matching unit or shelf opens straight away. Inbox and the bellRequests waiting on you and updates about your work both land in Inbox. The bell shows how many are unread.

A LAB station is a shared computer in the receiving lab. It stays signed in as LAB, and each person picks their own name so their work is recorded under it.

Start and end your turn

  1. On "Who is working here?", choose your name. Not listed? Choose Someone else… and find it.
  2. Work as usual. The top bar says "Recording as" with your name the whole time.
  3. When you leave, open your name and choose Sign off. The station goes back to "Who is working here?".
Is my Inbox shown on the station?Only if you turn it on: Settings, Preferences, Privacy, "Show my Inbox on shared LAB computers". It's off unless you choose it. Who can change what the station shows?Lab Managers. The change applies to everyone at that computer. Forgot the LAB password?Ask an administrator. A reset signs out every LAB computer.
